Everything You Need for Smooth Operations

Built specifically for retail stores, wholesalers, and fast-paced cash counters.

100% Offline-First

No internet connection required. Your business keeps operating seamlessly even during complete web network outages.

Inventory Tracking

Track stocks, low quantity alerts, unit conversions (Mund, Bags, KGs), and categories with sub-second lookups.

SQLite Database

Powered by reliable high-speed local storage. Supercharged transaction speeds with built-in backup tools.

Excel Import & Export

Import thousands of products from Excel sheets instantly and export detailed customer ledgers and daily sales reports.

Thermal Printing

Native ESC/POS thermal printer integration for 80mm and 58mm receipts, along with standard A4/A5 billing formats.

HotKey Optimized

Designed for fast keyboard-only operations (F1-F6 shortkeys) so cashier staff can clear customer queues effortlessly.
